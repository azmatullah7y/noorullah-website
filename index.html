About the source

Mohmand, once known chiefly for its mica and gem pegmatites, is home to some of KPK's tourmaline finds.

My name is Noor Ullah Khan. I live and work in Musa Khel, Upper Mohmand, in the pegmatite country of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa — one of the belts that also produces the emerald, aquamarine, and tourmaline Pakistan is known for internationally.

Every specimen listed here is handled personally, from the point it's brought down from the mine to the point it reaches you. I deal directly in raw and lightly-cleaned natural tourmaline and other crystal specimens — no lab-grown material, no resale of imported stock.
